# Search visibility tracking

Fill in from **Search Console → Performance → Search results** (filter by exact query, or by page). Leave a cell as `not available` rather than guessing. Average position is Google's reported average over the period, not a live rank; manual searches vary by location, device and personalization. Do not scrape Google results.

Check-ins: initial (first week with data), then ≈2, 4, 8 and 12 weeks after the v2.2 deploy, then monthly if useful.
