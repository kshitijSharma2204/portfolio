## Initial findings

1. Homepage experience cards show employer, role and dates, but no contribution summary.
2. All non-project pages shared the LiDAR project frame as their social preview image; the owner-supplied branded banner was not integrated.
3. Gallery categories are the original groups ("Robotics" holds only the SLAM project; EKF sits under "Planning and Control").
4. Project pages show the original description only; no case-study depth.
5. Gallery captions are generic ("Image 1").
6. The portrait sits in the lower About section of the homepage, not with the name.
7. Projects page HTML is ~149 KB uncompressed, mostly from server-rendered Quick View templates. Acceptable at this size (no regression introduced); left as is.
